Dataset: Evaluated on the IJCARS clinical B-mode liver ultrasound benchmark (55 patients, 550 raw scans) with self-supervised contrastive learning and spatial augmentations.

About the Fatty Liver Detection System

Non-Alcoholic Fatty Liver Disease (NAFLD) affects up to 25% of the global adult population and is strongly associated with metabolic syndrome, type 2 diabetes, and cardiovascular conditions. Early and accurate detection of hepatic steatosis is essential to halt or reverse progression toward non-alcoholic steatohepatitis (NASH), cirrhosis, or hepatocellular carcinoma.

While liver biopsy is the historic reference standard, it is invasive, costly, and associated with patient discomfort and complications. Ultrasound B-mode imaging is widely accessible and non-invasive, but visual qualitative evaluation suffers from inter-observer and intra-observer variability.

This clinical AI system implements a Siamese Neural Network with a ResNet-50 backbone trained via self-supervised contrastive learning. By extracting high-dimensional latent representations of liver parenchyma and ultrasound attenuation patterns, the model provides objective, reproducible assessments across all five clinical steatosis categories.

Clinical Usage Protocol

  1. Ensure the ultrasound image captures liver parenchyma adjacent to the right kidney or diaphragm.
  2. Verify the scan is in standard B-mode format (JPG, PNG, or BMP).
  3. Upload the scan or select one of the clinical reference samples.
  4. Review the multi-class probability distribution and confidence score.
  5. Export or print the report for medical records and radiologist corroboration.
